Key Strengths

Quality of Education 95/100

Exceptional teacher-student ratios and high learning outcome consistency across rural districts. Kerala maintains the highest literacy standards globally recognized.

Digital Access 91/100

Universal broadband connectivity in government schools and widespread availability of smart classrooms in both urban and hilly terrains.

Minor delays in fund utilization for new infrastructure projects and a need for streamlining inter-departmental data reporting cycles.

While school metrics are high, vocational track alignment and higher education transition rates show room for localized policy adjustment.
